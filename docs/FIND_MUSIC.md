# Where to find music

SPRAWL//RUN can play your own audio files during a run. The app has no internet
access and downloads nothing, so you get the files yourself, in a browser or on a
computer, and point the app at them.

## Music runners suggest

**Got a track or an album that keeps you running? [Tell us](https://github.com/jbinder/sprawlrun/issues/new?template=music_suggestion.yml), and your name goes next to it.** All it takes is a GitHub account, a link and a line on why you love it.

Seen one you'd run to? [Give its suggestion a 👍](https://github.com/jbinder/sprawlrun/issues?q=is%3Aissue+label%3Amusic). To narrow the list down, search this page for a word, such as `darksynth` or `long runs`.

Everything here is free to download, as far as its page said when it was added. Check that page for the licence and price as they are now. "All rights reserved" means free to download and listen to if the artist offers it so, but not to share or reuse. "Name your price" means you can enter 0, and may be asked for an email address. If you love it, consider paying. BPM figures are rough, and half-time music can read as double or half.

## Using a folder

1. Put your audio files in one folder on your phone. Files inside subfolders are
   not read.
2. In the app, go to **Settings → Audio → Run music** and choose **From a folder**.
3. Pick that folder. The app can only read that folder, and only needs Android's
   own folder permission.

It plays MP3, M4A, AAC, OGG, Opus, FLAC and WAV files in file-name order, or at
random if you switch that on. To control the order, start the names with numbers:
`01 Night Drive.mp3`, `02 Neon Rain.mp3`.

Story lines lower the music and bring it back. Unlike the app's own songs, a
recording can't change with your pace or a pursuit.

## Choosing music to run to

Music around your running cadence is easiest to run to, and that is usually
between 160 and 180 beats per minute. Many tracks at 80 to 90 BPM work too, since
you take two steps to each beat. Synthwave, darksynth, drum and bass, and techno
all suit the story. Try searching those words on the sites below.

Story lines play over your music, so if you follow the story, instrumental music
leaves the characters more room. Vocals work too, since the music drops for each line.

## Where to look

These sites offer music you can download for free, with the artist's permission.
**What you may do with a track differs from site to site and from track to track,
so read what its page says.** Listening on your own phone is about the least
anyone asks for, but sharing or reusing a track may not be allowed. We don't host
any of this music and can't vouch for what is on these sites. Some may ask you to
sign in before you can download.

| Site | What it is | Licence to check |
|---|---|---|
| [Free Music Archive](https://freemusicarchive.org/) | A large library, free to play, download and share for personal use. | Per track, often Creative Commons |
| [Jamendo Music](https://www.jamendo.com/) | Independent artists, free for personal listening and download. | Per track or album, set by the artist |
| [Internet Archive: Netlabels](https://archive.org/details/netlabels) | Whole catalogues from non-profit online labels, strong on techno and electronic music. | Per release, often Creative Commons |
| [OpenGameArt](https://opengameart.org/) | Music made for games. Search for "synthwave" or "cyberpunk". | Per track, often CC0 or CC-BY |
| [ccMixter](https://ccmixter.org/) | A remix community, searched through [dig.ccMixter](https://dig.ccmixter.org/). | Per track, Creative Commons |
| [Bandcamp](https://bandcamp.com/tag/synthwave) | Artists sell here directly. Some let you name your price, including nothing. If you like them, pay. | Set by the artist |

Music you already own works just as well, if you can copy it as plain files. Tracks
protected by a streaming service's app can't be played from a folder.

## Add a site

Know another good place for free music to run to? Open a pull request on this page,
or [suggest it as an idea](https://github.com/jbinder/sprawlrun/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml).
It should be free to download with the artist's permission, and clear about its
licences.
